# Addendum — Leaf Me Alone Game Brief

Depth preserved for GDD (`gds-gdd`) and prototype work. Not required reading for the brief itself.

---

## Coach Rationales (Vision)

**Core fantasy choice:** Strategist and "nature fights back" describe how the game plays and what the world represents — not the hook. The hook is emotional plants that can desert you. Caretaker-commander merges placement/planning with water, fertilize, and mood management.

**Emotional takeaway choice:** Pure strategist satisfaction → generic TD. Pure guilt → bummer. Steam pitch lives in contrast: chibi apes in vests doing HR outreach while your Cashew rage-quits. Tagline cynicism is worldview flavor, not the dominant post-session feeling.

---

## Session Length Tension

Brainstorm: 5 waves × 5–10 min (~25–50 min). Brief target: 15–30 min per run. Resolution: prototype at ~3–5 min combat + Pause per wave; validate in vertical slice.

---

## Player–Experience Goals (full)

| Mechanic | Emotion |
|---|---|
| Flee on foot | Surprise → amusement or guilt |
| Care during Pause | Agency — "I should have watered that Teak" |
| Corporate ape waves | Cynical delight — recognition of satire |
| Card picks with risk | Tension — power now vs. mood later |
| Random biome/boss | Discovery — "one more run" |
| Biology-accurate plants | Satisfying depth — smart placement pays off |

## Comparable Titles (extended)

| Title | What we take | What we deliberately leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Plants vs. Zombies** | Plant theme, humor, readable TD | Lane-based layout; pure good-vs-evil framing |
| **Bloons TD 6** | Wave mastery, upgrade satisfaction, premium PC model | Hero towers, deep persistent upgrade trees, no mood/flee |
| **Slay the Spire** | Between-encounter card picks, run variance, meta unlocks | Turn-based combat; no spatial TD placement |
| **Emberward / Rogue Tower** | TD + roguelite hybrid on Steam, $15 tier | No ecosystem mood; no satirical enemy faction |
| **Plant the Towers** | Day/night TD + farming, nutrient balance | Apocalyptic bugs; no flee/desertion mechanic |
| **Gardener's Luck** | Mobile plant units, roguelike picks | Gambling machine core loop; not satire-focused |
| **They Are Billions** | Horde pressure, base strongholds | Real-time colony sim scale; grim tone |
| **Ratropolis** | Cards + defense hybrid | City-deckbuilder APM; rats not plants |
| **Overbloom / Galactic Gardener / Seeb Defender** | Grid TD roguelite, plant themes | No flee, corporate satire, or biology-driven soil roles |

3. **Greenwash Fail** — Destroy all PR Ape billboards

5. **Garden of Seven Sins** — Activate all 7 Deadly Sins in one run

## Red Soil — 7 Deadly Sins (in-run, post-slice)

| Sin | Plant | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pride | Teak | Never flees; DEF ×2; on death = 3×3 zone loses red soil buff |
| Gluttony | Cashew | Devours low-HP apes; grows fast; red soil depleted after 3 waves |
| Lust | Peanut | Uncontrolled spread; auto-claims empty tiles each Pause |
| Envy | Cashew | Debuff non-red-soil plants; +50% buff to adjacent red soil plants |
| Wrath | Teak | Reflect damage ×3; cannot be cared for; cannot hide |
| Sloth | Peanut | Auto-hides entire first Pause; no attack for 2 waves |
| Greed | Cashew | Steals all tile buffs; tile becomes barren permanently |

Design Sand, Rock, Mold clan sins after Red Soil proves fun in playtest.
